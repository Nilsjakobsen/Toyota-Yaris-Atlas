## Conflicts between sources

* **74 → 73 kW date:** ADAC says 10/2010; other secondary sources say September 2010
  or 2011.
* **XP90 CO₂:** the Feb 2009 release gives 119 g/km for both gearboxes (120 for SR);
  the July 2009 brochure and ADAC give 120 manual / 119 MultiMode.
* **Rear brakes:** UK drums (discs with VSC) vs ADAC discs.
* **End of XP90 production:** May 2011 (Wikipedia) vs 2011.07 (parts catalogue).
* **Kerb weight:** UK lists 1,040–1,080 kg; ADAC 1,115 kg (EU method includes the
  driver). Your 1070 kg does not discriminate.

## Open questions, in order of usefulness

~~Gearbox~~: answered 3 October 2026, **manual**.

1. **Model code on the vehicle label** (expected NSP90…). Confirms generation and
   engine in one go.
2. **Rear brakes**, drum or disc. Look through a rear wheel.
3. **VSC fitted?** Look for a VSC OFF button or warning lamp at ignition-on.
4. **Stop & Start?** Does the engine stop by itself at traffic lights?
5. **Owner's manual** for your exact variant. It is the right source for warning lamps,
   fuses, fluids and jacking points.
6. Tyre sidewall marking and the placard's recommended pressures.
7. Battery, fuse box, reservoir and OBD socket locations. Mark each one "found on my
   car" in the Inspector.

Answer 1–4 and 6 in the app (Vehicle → Open questions). The answers are stored locally
and included in log exports.

## Deliberately not included

Part numbers, torque settings, fluid specifications, fuse assignments, wiring,
service intervals and exact component locations. They need documentation matched to
your exact variant (owner's manual, Toyota service information), and guessing them is
worse than leaving them out.
